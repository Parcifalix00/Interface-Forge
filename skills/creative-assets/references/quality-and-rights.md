# Quality, provenance, and rights

Inspect the actual output at final usage sizes: text, edges, contrast, alignment, aspect ratio, crop, background, and small-scale readability. A correct prompt is not sufficient. If you cannot inspect the image, hand it off for review without recording a visual `PASS`.

Record origin, known author/provider, license or permission, and modifications. Open-source instructions do not grant rights to arbitrary photos or logos. Do not claim legal originality, exclusivity, or trademark registrability without professional review.

Do not distribute font files. Do not embed remotely tracked images without an explicit decision. Treat external SVG files as untrusted content. Inspect scripts, remote references, and active content before use and do not execute them merely to inspect them.

Provide relevant alt text or decorative semantics according to the asset role. Do not unnecessarily duplicate a control label. Provide a textual equivalent for informational diagrams. Optimize weight and format without destroying transparency, text, or necessary detail.

The manifest distinguishes proposed, approved, and final assets. Successful generation does not equal user selection and does not guarantee pixel-perfect invariants.
