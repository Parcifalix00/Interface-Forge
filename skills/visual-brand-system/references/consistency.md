# Consistency and dependencies

For every output, record foundation revision, role, layout, format, and specific decisions. Reuse canonical files rather than redrawing them. Verify text, proportions, colors, fonts actually loaded, and light/dark variants when supported.

A change to one slot creates a review list for affected derivatives. It does not mean every derivative is wrong. A font change does not necessarily alter a geometric symbol. Keep dependencies explicit and evaluate them case by case.

To correct an output, change the minimum necessary and then review the entire affected area. If the correction changes identity or approved copy, return to the corresponding decision rather than inventing a technical approval.

Always hand off status: approved, proposed, needs review, or superseded. The templates do not provide a database or executable state machine. Do not promise automatic enforcement.
