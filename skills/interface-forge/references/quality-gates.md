# Quality gates

## G0: scope
The task, constraints, and targets are explicit or assumptions are clearly marked. No unauthorized spending, publishing, or migration.

## G1: product and content
The UI matches the requested product. Existing brand, supplied copy, and data are respected. No invented metrics or decorative-only interactions presented as functional.

## G2: behavior
Changed flows are exercised across their relevant states. Controls are not inert. Mocks are clearly marked and confined to the requested prototype.

## G3: visual quality and accessibility
Compare against an approved specification or real baseline. Check readability, spacing, focus, contrast, input behavior, and long content. Authored copy does not introduce em dashes. Automated tests are only one part of verification.

## G4: platforms
Every target family has results tied to the actual environment. Emulation and real devices are not synonyms. Missing required evidence: INCOMPLETE. Material defects: BLOCKED. All required checks passed or excluded with rationale: READY_FOR_REVIEW, not universal certification.

## G5: handoff
Files, evidence, limitations, and pending decisions are readable. No implicit commit or deploy. Do not repeat the entire documentation set in the final response.
